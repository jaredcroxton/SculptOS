Paid Search
Dominate Google. Top to Bottom.
Local Services Ads + Search Ads. Two channels, one local-domination strategy.
Local Services Ads (LSA)
Above All Other Results
Google Guarantee badge included. Instant credibility, premium placement.
Pay per lead, not per click. Far more predictable budgeting.
30-40% lower cost per lead vs traditional PPC in home services.
Application: background check + ARCtick + QBCC licence verification.
Define service areas, job types and weekly budget cap.
Google ranks fast responders. Reply within minutes, not hours.
Google Search Ads
Target High-Intent Keywords
Intent keywords: "AC not cooling Sunshine Coast", "ducted repair Noosa".
Use every ad extension: call, location, site-links, structured snippets.
Geo-target service areas only. Zero wasted spend on outside clicks.
Import GA4 conversion events directly into Google Ads.
Cut keywords with traffic but no calls. Reinvest in what converts.
Emergency keywords cost more per click but convert at the highest rate.
LSA + Search Ads together place Atkinsons Air at the very top of Google for every high-intent HVAC search on the Sunshine Coast. Above competitors running only one channel.
