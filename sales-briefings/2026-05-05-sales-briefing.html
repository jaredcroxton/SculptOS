Braun's framework has four parts: Trigger (a specific, relevant observation about the prospect, not generic flattery), Think (an open question about how they handle the challenge you spotted), Third-Party Credibility (one sentence of social proof from a similar buyer, not a feature list), and Talk (a low-friction ask, not "book a 30-minute call"). Example for your outreach: "Trigger: I noticed you were at the Skift Asia Forum in Bangkok last week. Think: I'm curious how rate compression events like Golden Week are hitting your APAC hotel spend. 2. APAC Angles

Australia

Sydney hotel pricing data from RevPARGenius shows a clean weekday ADR of $140.25 and clean weekend ADR of $199.00 as of early May 2026, with weekend demand materially outpacing weekday demand before Vivid Sydney has even started. The event runs May 22-June 13 (23 nights). Based on 2025 performance, compression during the event is near-90% weekend occupancy. Australian hotel industry achieved record performance in 2025 and maintains a positive outlook for 2026, with occupancy-driven growth across major markets. Sales move: share the Accor Vivid Sydney member booking page directly with NSW and Sydney prospects this week. Frame it as "the best rate windows narrow from May 22, booking inside member rates now is the straightforward call." The weekday-to-weekend ADR gap ($58.75) is also a strong ROI data point for members who travel for business during the week and leisure on weekends. (RevPARGenius, May 2026; Hotel News Resource, 2026)

New Zealand

International arrivals in early 2026 have surpassed 2019 benchmarks across all major NZ markets for the first time. Auckland, Queenstown, and Christchurch ADRs are up double-digits year on year. Queenstown remains the most constrained market by geography, and premium repositioning is accelerating: Waimarino Lodge (20 exclusive villas near Lake Wakatipu) opens in 2026, Radisson RED Queenstown has been signed for early 2028, and Marriott's St. Regis Queenstown is planned for 2027. Each new premium opening raises the baseline rate at comparable Accor properties by compressing supply at the luxury tier. Sales move: for NZ prospects, the "premium repositioning" narrative works well. "Queenstown ADRs are up double-digits this year and the luxury pipeline for 2027-28 is significant. Our member rate gives you a locked-in discount before those new properties arrive and reset the market ceiling upward." (Travel and Tour World, 2026; Latte Luxury News, Mar 4)

India

India's hotel market is running on three strong tailwinds simultaneously: hotel room rates rising 6% in 2026 (ARR in the ₹9,400-₹9,700 range), the India MICE market worth USD 42.25 billion growing at an 11.92% CAGR, and corporate travel budgets now at 110% of pre-pandemic levels with technology, pharma, and financial-services firms accounting for 40%+ of corporate MICE. Convention infrastructure is maturing fast: Jio World Convention Centre, Yashobhoomi, and Bharat Mandapam are all anchoring incremental MICE demand. Hotel pipeline growth is 31% year on year (906 projects). Sales move: India is a MICE-first pitch market. "Corporate travel budgets in India are at 110% of pre-pandemic levels and room rates are rising. Our member rate is locked in regardless of how ARR moves over the year. If your team is in Bengaluru, Mumbai, or Delhi for conferences and MICE events, the savings compound quickly. Let's run the numbers on your travel calendar." Target professionals in tech, financial services, and pharma. (Travel and Tour World, 2026; Mordor Intelligence, 2026)

Thailand

Thailand is entering its low season (May-October), with monsoon pricing typically running 30-50% below peak. YTD arrivals through April 19 were 10.83 million (-3.34% year on year), and the Thai Hotels Association expects full-year arrivals around 33 million, broadly flat with 2025. The Tourism Authority is explicitly targeting high-value, long-stay visitors rather than volume. For sellers, this is actually a buying-signal environment for prospects: "Hotels in Thailand are running aggressive low-season pricing right now. That compression will reverse in November when high season starts. Our member rate gives you the low-season discount locked in throughout the year, not just when hotels are competing hardest. When peak season arrives, your discount is worth significantly more than it is today." Target prospects planning Q4 Thailand travel who can book now at member rates. (Adasia Consulting, 2026; Go2Thailand, 2026)

Indonesia

Bali hotel occupancy averaged 73.2% in 2025 (easing 2.5% from 2024's elevated level), with the mid-market segment (USD 81-140/night) delivering the strongest performance. International arrivals hit 6.95 million in 2025, a new all-time high and the second consecutive record year. The active hotel pipeline in Bali stands at 5,641 rooms across 45 hotels in Canggu, Jimbaran/Uluwatu, and Ubud, with new luxury supply arriving through 2026-2027. Indonesia's March 31 OTA licensing enforcement (unregistered villas removed from booking platforms) has compressed informal supply competition in Bali and Jakarta, improving the relative value of branded Accor properties. Sales move: "Bali is running at record arrivals and the supply of unlicensed villas on Airbnb and Booking is being cut. The value case for staying at an Accor property with member rates has improved significantly this year. Let me show you what that looks like for your next trip." (Hotel News Resource / Horwath HTL, 2026; Hotelier Indonesia, Apr 2026)

5. Tactics This Week

Outbound Braun 4-T email: Trigger, Think, Third-Party, Talk, in 80 words or fewer. Josh Braun's cold email framework strips everything unnecessary. Each element in one sentence. Trigger: specific observation (event, role change, market news). Think: open question about the challenge. Third-Party: one social proof sentence from a similar buyer. Talk: low-friction ask ("worth a reply?"). Accor Plus application: Trigger = "Vietnam hotels are running at 75% occupancy right now." Think = "I'm curious how rate compression is affecting your APAC hotel spend." Third-Party = "We help frequent APAC travelers lock in 15-20% member rates across seven markets." Talk = "Worth a quick reply if relevant?" Write three versions of this today. Test. (Josh Braun, joshbraun.com, 2026)
Discovery Gong discovery principle: talk less than 40% of the call, ask more than you answer. Gong's data from 7.1 million deals is unambiguous: the discovery call determines the outcome, not the closing call. Reps who dominate the conversation in discovery lose more deals than reps who flip the ratio. Target questions for Accor discovery: "What APAC markets did you visit in the last 12 months?" / "Were any of those trips during compression events like Golden Week or Vivid?" / "What do you typically pay per night at a 4-5 star property in Bangkok or Sydney?" / "Have you ever run the math on what you spend on hotels in a year versus what a membership would cost?" Each question generates data you use to close. Let the prospect do the ROI work. (Gong Labs, 2026)
Closing Double-deadline close: Hyatt May 20 and Vivid Sydney May 22, both live this week. When a prospect is in late discovery and the ROI math works: don't ask if they want to join. Assume the yes, offer a date. "Given your travel to [Sydney/India/Vietnam], the numbers clearly work in your favor. There are two things happening in the next two weeks that make doing this now smarter than next month. Shall we sort it today while I'm on the line, or is Thursday a better day?" The binary choice (today or Thursday) outperforms open-ended questions because it removes the decision of whether to proceed and replaces it with the easier decision of when. (HubSpot Sales Blog, 2026)
Negotiation Price defense: shift from cost language to hedge language. "It's expensive" is rarely a budget objection. It is a value-clarity objection. When you hear it: don't defend the price. Label the concern first, then redirect to the hedge framing. "It sounds like you're not sure the savings would be significant enough on your travel volume. Let me show you the math." Then: "At 15% off a $200/night room, you recover the membership inside 6-8 nights. Beyond that, you're also hedging against what Hyatt and IHG are doing to their redemption programmes right now. What part of the value feels unclear to you?" The label (sounds like you're not sure) reduces defensiveness before the data lands. (Gong.io, 2026; Black Swan Group / Chris Voss, 2026)
Prospecting AI prep generates 77% more revenue: build discovery questions with AI before every call. Gong's data shows sellers who actively use AI in their sales process generate 77% more revenue than those who don't. The highest-leverage AI use for your role is pre-call research and question building. Before each Accor Plus call, use AI to: (1) pull the prospect's APAC travel markets from their LinkedIn/company profile, (2) find the most recent hotel occupancy or ADR data point for those markets, (3) generate 5 specific discovery questions tailored to their industry and travel footprint. This takes 3-4 minutes per call and gives you the material for a Gong-style discovery conversation. Concrete next action: use this for your next 5 calls this week, note which questions generated the most engagement. (Gong Labs, 2026; Salesloft, 2026)
LinkedIn Justin Welsh's 2026 LinkedIn algorithm: proof-based hooks and 6-7 posts per week. Welsh and Donnelly analyzed 397,605 posts for Q1 2026 and found the algorithm has shifted significantly. The old playbook (3x week, opinion-based hooks) underperforms. What works now: 6-7 posts per week, hooks with authority and receipts ("Vietnam hotels are running at 75% occupancy. Here's what that means for APAC travelers..."), niche-specific content that lets the algorithm find the right audience. Objection Consensus-partner framework for corporate accounts: different message for each stakeholder. Morgan Ingram's enterprise approach maps three separate value threads before asking for anything: the EA/travel coordinator (cares about booking simplicity and policy compliance), the CFO/finance lead (cares about spend reduction and predictability), and the actual traveler (cares about convenience, upgrades, and status). Most Accor Plus pitches go to the traveler and stop there. The CFO angle: "Corporate hotel spend in India is up 6% this year. Our membership converts variable hotel costs into a fixed member rate with measurable savings across your APAC travel program." The EA angle: "Your team books across 7 APAC markets. One membership covers them all under a single discounted rate. No per-booking registration or promo codes to track." (Morgan Ingram, LinkedIn, 2026)
