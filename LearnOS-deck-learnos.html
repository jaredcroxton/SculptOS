07 · Pocket Customer

Your team practises with the customer,
not the script.

Until now, the only way to practise a difficult customer call was to have one. We changed that. An AI customer your reps can practise against — by typing, or out loud, in their own voice.

Voice mode
Spoken roleplay, sub-second

OpenAI Realtime, whisper-1 transcription, six voice picks (cedar, ash, ballad, marin, coral, sage). Turn detection tuned to 900 ms.

Text mode
40-turn deep practise

gpt-4o-mini powers the conversation. Reinforcement prompting so the persona never drifts. Redo any session at any time.

Library
Real scenarios, extensible

Win-backs, renewals, missing-points recovery, hotel check-in upsells, cancellations. Managers assign scenarios as homework.

13 · The pricing story

Pay for what your team
actually uses.

A traditional LMS sells you seats. LearnOS sells you the minutes your team actually learns.

Traditional LMS

  • Per-seat licence, paid whether anyone logs in or not
  • Multi-year contracts, paid up front
  • Per-language fee with a translation agency
  • Per-call human roleplay coach, if you can find one
  • Monthly minimums regardless of usage

LearnOS

  • No per-seat fee. Idle users cost nothing
  • Month-to-month spend, scales with usage
  • Cents-per-step translation, only on first run
  • AI roleplay metered by minutes spoken
  • No monthly minimum, no floor, no lock-in

Three pass-through costs: OpenAI tokens, Supabase usage, Vercel hosting. That's the bill. There is no fourth line.
