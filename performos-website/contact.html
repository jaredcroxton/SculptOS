What happens on the call

A short, focused conversation to give you an honest read. No slide deck, no rehearsed pitch.

  • 01
    You describe your business What you do, team size, current AI use, top frustrations.
  • 02
    We listen and ask We dig into the workflows that take the most time and the ones that cost the most when they go wrong.
  • 03
    You get a read An honest opinion on where to start, whether that is training, implementation, software, or none of the above yet.
  • 04
    You decide If there is a fit, we send a scope and pricing. If there is not, we tell you and recommend something else.
