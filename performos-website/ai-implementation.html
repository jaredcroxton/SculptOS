Our process

How a PerformOS implementation actually runs

A four-step process that gets one workflow live, documented, and adopted. No theatre.

1

Audit

We map the work that costs you the most time. Together we shortlist one or two workflows worth rebuilding.

2

Design

We choose tools, draft the new flow, and identify what stays human, what becomes AI-assisted, and what becomes automatic.

3

Build

We configure tools, write prompts, integrate where needed, and test using your real data and inputs.

4

Roll out

We document the workflow, train the team, and run a short feedback loop in the first week to tune what is not landing.

Timeline

Common questions about AI implementation

It involves four stages: audit your current workflows, choose a small set of tools, build and test the new AI-assisted workflow, then roll it out with training. The goal is one or two working workflows live within a sprint, not a giant transformation programme.

Start with high-volume, low-judgement work. Common first wins are first-draft emails, proposals, meeting notes, internal knowledge access, customer FAQ responses, and onboarding documents. Avoid anything tied to legal, financial, or compliance decisions in the first wave.

A focused workflow build typically takes 2 to 6 weeks from scoping to live, depending on complexity. A wider 30 to 90 day plan can sequence several workflows safely, with training and policy woven in.
